   - `--date-field` - Choose which date field to filter on (created, updated, last_modified, last_indexed)
   - Full integration with existing filters (`--title`, `--author`, `--type`, `--limit`)

- **Multiple Date Fields**: `zinfo query --after 2024-01 --date-field created`
